Hello from Maria

Made locally, served warmly

Café Rosa is a small independent coffee shop and brunch spot on the high street. Maria started the café to create the kind of place where neighbours, office workers and friends could stop for a proper coffee and something homemade.

We keep things simple: good ingredients, friendly service and food that feels at home. Whether you're grabbing coffee on your commute or staying for brunch, we're happy to have you here.
